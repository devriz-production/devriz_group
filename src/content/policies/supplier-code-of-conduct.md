Devriz Group and its companies want to work with vendors, contractors and partners who run their businesses lawfully, safely and honestly. This code sets out the minimum we expect.

## Who this code applies to

The code applies to every supplier of goods or services to Devriz Group or a group company: vendors, contractors, subcontractors, consultants, service providers, agents and business partners, and the people they employ on our work. Where the law or a contract sets a higher standard, the higher standard applies.

## Legal compliance

Suppliers must follow all laws that apply to their business, including labour, tax, safety, environmental and licensing laws. They must hold the licences, registrations and permits their work requires, and show them when asked.

## Labour and working conditions

- **No child labour.** Suppliers must not employ children below the minimum age set by law. No one under 18 may do hazardous work.
- **No forced labour.** All work must be voluntary. Bonded, trafficked or involuntary labour is not acceptable. Suppliers must not hold workers' original identity documents or withhold wages as a means of control.
- **Fair wages.** Workers must be paid at least the legal minimum wage, in full and on time, with the benefits and contributions the law requires. Unlawful deductions are not allowed.
- **Working hours.** Working hours, rest days, leave and overtime pay must follow the law.
- **Respect.** Workers must be treated with dignity, free from discrimination, harassment, abuse and physical punishment.

## Health and safety

Suppliers must provide a safe and healthy workplace. On construction sites and other high-risk work, we expect suppliers to:

- assess the risks before work starts and brief workers on them
- give workers suitable protective equipment at no cost to them, and make sure it is used
- use trained and competent people for work at height, excavation, electrical work, lifting and machinery
- provide safe access, drinking water, sanitation and first aid
- report every accident and serious near miss on our sites to us promptly
- let workers stop work that is unsafe, without penalty

## Anti-bribery and fair competition

- Suppliers must not offer, give or accept bribes, kickbacks or unofficial payments in connection with our business. This applies to our employees, public officials and anyone else.
- Suppliers must not offer cash, or any gift meant to influence a decision, to our employees or their families.
- Suppliers must not rig bids, fix prices or share markets with competitors.
- Quotations, invoices and supporting documents must be genuine and accurate.

## Conflicts of interest

Suppliers must tell us, before bidding or as soon as it arises, about any financial or personal relationship with one of our employees or directors, or their family members, that could influence a business decision.

## Quality and product safety

Goods and services must meet the agreed specification, the applicable standards and the law. This is critical for healthcare items, food products, construction materials and safety equipment.

- Do not substitute materials or change a specification without our written approval.
- Do not supply counterfeit, expired, adulterated or mislabelled goods.
- Tell us at once if you find a defect or a safety risk in something you have supplied.

## Environmental responsibility

Suppliers must follow environmental law and hold the consents their work requires. They must handle waste, emissions and hazardous materials responsibly, control dust, noise and debris at work sites, avoid wasting water, energy and materials, and never dump waste illegally.

## Confidentiality and data protection

Suppliers must keep our business information confidential and use it only for the work agreed.

Where a supplier handles personal data for us, it must use the data only as we instruct, keep it secure, comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and tell us promptly about any breach.

Suppliers must not use the Devriz Group name or logo, or those of group companies, without written permission.

## Subcontracting

Suppliers must not pass our work to a subcontractor without our prior written approval where the contract requires it. The supplier remains responsible for the work and must make sure every subcontractor meets the standards in this code.

## Record keeping and right to verify

Suppliers must keep accurate records that show they meet this code, such as wage and attendance registers, licences and safety records. With reasonable notice, we may ask for information, visit a site or review relevant records to check compliance.

## Raising concerns

Suppliers and their workers can report a suspected breach of this code, including by one of our own employees, using the contact details below. We do not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith, and suppliers must not retaliate against workers who do so.

## Consequences

If a supplier falls short of this code, we may ask for a corrective plan with clear dates. For a serious or repeated breach we may suspend orders, end the contract or remove the supplier from our vendor list, as the contract and the law allow. Where the law requires it, matters are reported to the authorities.

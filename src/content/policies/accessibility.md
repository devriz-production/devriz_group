Devriz Group wants everyone to be able to read and use this website. That includes people who use a screen reader, people who use a keyboard instead of a mouse, and people who need larger text, stronger contrast or less movement on screen.

## Our commitment

We treat accessibility as part of building the website properly, not as an extra. We work to remove barriers when we find them, and we want to hear about the ones we have missed.

## The standard we follow

The website is built to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. This is the standard we design and build to. It is a working target. We are not claiming that the site has been independently audited or certified against it.

## What we have done

- **Skip to content link.** Keyboard and screen reader users can jump past the navigation to the main content of each page.
- **Keyboard navigation.** Every link, button, menu and form can be reached and used with the keyboard alone, and the focused item is clearly visible.
- **Text-size controls.** The A-, A and A+ controls make the text smaller or larger, or return it to the default size.
- **High-contrast mode.** A high-contrast setting strengthens the difference between text and background.
- **Light and dark themes.** You can choose the theme that is more comfortable for you.
- **Reduced motion.** If your device is set to reduce motion, the website respects that setting.
- **Alt text.** Meaningful images have text alternatives.
- **Clear structure.** Pages use semantic headings and landmarks, so assistive technology can move through them in a logical order.
- **Accessible forms.** Form fields have labels, and errors are shown as inline messages next to the field they relate to.

Your theme, contrast and text-size choices are saved in your own browser only. Our Cookie policy explains this.

## Known limitations

We know that some parts of the website are not yet fully accessible:

- **Press logos.** Some logos of publications that have covered the group may not be fully accessible.
- **Older PDFs.** Some older PDF documents may not work well with screen readers.
- **Third-party embedded content.** Content from other providers, such as a map on the contact page, is controlled by that provider and may not meet the same standard. It loads only if you choose to open it. Our office addresses are also given as plain text in the Contact section below.
- **Hindi.** A full Hindi version of the website is not yet available. A [Hindi overview page](/hi/) is available.

If one of these limitations stops you from getting information you need, please contact us. We will try to give you the information in another way.

## How to give feedback

If you find a barrier on this website, or have a suggestion, please tell us:

- by email to [info@devrizgroup.com](mailto:info@devrizgroup.com), with the subject line "Accessibility feedback"
- by phone on +91 97737 79315
- by post to the corporate office address given below

It helps us if you include:

- the address of the page, or a description of where you were on the website
- what you were trying to do and what went wrong
- the device, browser and any assistive technology you were using
- how you would like us to reply

## How we handle feedback

- We read every message about accessibility and pass it to the team that maintains the website.
- Where a fix is possible, we plan it into our work on the website.
- Where a fix is not possible straight away, we tell you, and we try to provide the information you need in another way.
- If you are not satisfied with our reply, or you want your concern handled formally, you can raise it under our Grievance redressal process. That page sets out the steps and the response times we aim for.

We review this statement when the website changes in ways that affect accessibility.

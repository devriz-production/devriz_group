Devriz Group builds and operates companies across healthcare, security, construction, consumer products, agriculture, logistics and education. This code sets out the behaviour we expect from everyone who works under the group's name.

It rests on three values: discipline before expansion, execution before promises, systems before shortcuts.

## Who this code applies to

The code applies to everyone who works for or represents Devriz Group or a group company: employees at every level, including trainees and interns, directors, and consultants, agents and others who act on our behalf.

Vendors and contractors are covered by our Supplier code of conduct. Where the law or a group company policy sets a stricter rule, follow the stricter rule.

## Integrity and honesty

- Tell the truth to colleagues, customers, partners and regulators.
- Do not promise what you cannot deliver.
- Own your mistakes and report them early.

## Compliance with law

Follow the laws, licences and professional standards that apply to your work. This matters most in regulated activities such as healthcare, security services, construction, food and transport. If you are unsure whether something is lawful, ask before you act. No business target justifies breaking the law.

## Conflicts of interest

A conflict arises when a personal interest, relationship or outside activity could affect your judgement at work, or could appear to. Examples include a financial interest in a supplier or competitor, awarding work to a relative, and outside work that competes with a group company.

Disclose any conflict in writing to your manager or HR, and step away from the decision until it is resolved.

## Gifts and anti-bribery

- Never offer, give, ask for or accept a bribe, kickback or other improper advantage, directly or through someone else. This applies to public officials and private parties alike.
- Do not make unofficial payments to speed up a routine approval.
- Gifts and hospitality must be modest, occasional and openly given. Never give or accept cash, and never exchange gifts while a tender, contract or approval is pending.

## Fair dealing

- **Customers.** Describe products and services accurately and keep the commitments we make.
- **Patients.** Safety, dignity, consent and confidentiality come first. Clinical decisions are made by qualified professionals.
- **Students.** Give honest information about courses, fees and outcomes. Do not make promises we cannot keep.
- **Suppliers.** Select on merit, treat them fairly and honour agreed terms.

## Respect at work

Treat every person with dignity. We do not accept discrimination on the basis of religion, caste, gender, age, disability, region, language or any other ground protected by law. Bullying, threats, abuse and violence have no place at work.

### Prevention of sexual harassment

Sexual harassment in any form is not tolerated. We follow the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013. Complaints are handled by the Internal Committee of the relevant group company, as the Act provides, and are treated confidentially. No one may retaliate against a complainant or a witness.

## Health and safety

On every site, in every clinic and office, and on the road:

- Follow safety rules and use the protective equipment provided.
- Stop and report any work that looks unsafe.
- Report hazards, accidents and near misses promptly.
- Never work under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

## Confidential information and personal data

Protect business information such as plans, pricing and contracts.

Handle the personal data of customers, patients, students, employees and applicants with care. Collect only what is needed, use it only for the purpose it was given, keep it secure and share it only with authorised people. Follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and our Privacy policy.

These duties continue after you leave the group.

## Accurate records and reporting

Financial, operational, clinical, safety and attendance records must be complete, accurate and timely. Do not falsify or backdate a record, hide a transaction or destroy records needed for an audit, investigation or legal matter.

## Use of company assets

Use company money, equipment, vehicles, materials, systems and brand names for company business, and protect them from theft, waste and misuse. Speak to the media or post on behalf of the group only if you are authorised to do so.

## Speaking up and non-retaliation

If you see or suspect a breach of this code, speak up. You can raise it with your manager, with HR or with the corporate office.

- Concerns raised in good faith are protected. Retaliation against anyone who speaks up is itself a breach of this code.
- We keep reports as confidential as the law and a fair review allow.
- Knowingly making a false report is a breach of this code.

## Consequences of breach

A breach of this code may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including the end of employment or engagement, in line with company policy and applicable law. Where the law requires it, matters are reported to the authorities. Managers who knew of a breach and ignored it may also be held accountable.

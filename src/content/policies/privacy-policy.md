Devriz Group ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use devrizgroup.com or contact us, why we collect it, how we protect it and what rights you have.

We handle personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the DPDP Act) and other applicable Indian laws. If you visit from outside India, you may have rights under your local data protection law. We will make reasonable efforts to respect those rights where they apply.

## Terms used in this policy

- **Personal data**: any data about an individual who can be identified by or in relation to that data.
- **Data Principal**: the individual the personal data relates to. For a child, or a person with a disability who has a lawful guardian, this includes the parent or lawful guardian.
- **Data Fiduciary**: Devriz Group, which decides why and how personal data is processed.
- **Data Processor**: a person or organisation that processes personal data on our behalf.

## What we collect

### Information you give us

This website is informational. It has no user accounts, payments or comments. We collect personal data only when you choose to send it to us through:

- the general contact, partnership enquiry, vendor registration, media enquiry and feedback forms
- the careers application form
- email, telephone or post

Depending on the form, this may include your name, email address, mobile number, city, organisation name and the details of your enquiry. A careers application also asks for the role you are applying for, your experience, salary expectations, notice period, links to your resume and portfolio, and a short summary of your work.

Form submissions are sent by email to the Devriz Group corporate team.

### Information recorded automatically

- **Server logs.** Like most websites, the servers that host this site may record your IP address, your browser type and the pages you request. These logs are used for security.
- **No tracking cookies.** The site does not set advertising or analytics cookies.
- **Preferences on your device.** Your choice of colour theme, high-contrast mode and text size is saved in your browser's local storage. These settings never leave your device. Our Cookie policy lists them.
- **Embedded content.** Third-party content, such as a map on the contact page, loads only if you choose to open it. Once opened, that provider may collect data under its own privacy policy.

If we add analytics in future, we will update this policy and the Cookie policy first.

## Why we use personal data

We use personal data to:

- respond to enquiries, requests and other communications
- process applications for jobs, internships and other roles
- send updates or newsletters, only where you have given prior consent
- manage our relationship with you, including telling you about changes to our policies
- keep the website secure and working properly
- meet legal, regulatory and contractual obligations

## Our legal basis

We process personal data with your consent where we ask for it, and under the legitimate uses set out in Section 7 of the DPDP Act. These include:

- personal data you voluntarily give us for a specified purpose
- compliance with an obligation under applicable law
- compliance with a judgment, decree or order of a court or authority
- employment-related purposes, including recruitment and human resources functions
- responding to a medical, health or public emergency as required by law
- processing needed for disaster management or a breakdown of public order

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time by writing to us. If we plan to use your personal data for a new purpose, we will tell you in advance where the law requires it.

## Children

This website is not intended for children, meaning anyone who has not completed 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect or process the personal data of children. Where needed, we may ask visitors to confirm their age. If we find that we have collected a child's personal data by mistake, we will delete it promptly.

## How we share personal data

We do not sell personal data or exploit it commercially. We may share it:

- within Devriz Group companies, for legitimate business purposes such as passing your enquiry to the right team
- with trusted service providers who act as Data Processors for us, for example for website hosting and email, under confidentiality obligations
- with courts, regulators or other authorities, where the law requires it

## Social media

We run official pages on social media platforms. Anything you post publicly there is governed by the privacy policy of that platform. We are not responsible for content on third-party platforms unless it is published by our authorised representatives.

## Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have the right to:

- **Access**: ask for the personal data we hold about you and how we process it.
- **Correction and erasure**: ask us to correct inaccurate data, or to erase personal data in certain circumstances.
- **Grievance redressal**: raise a concern about how we handle your personal data and have it addressed. Our Grievance redressal page explains the process.
- **Nomination**: nominate another individual to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated, subject to verification.

To use any of these rights, email [info@devrizgroup.com](mailto:info@devrizgroup.com) with the subject line "Privacy request". We may need to verify your identity first. We aim to respond to data-rights requests within 30 days.

If you are still not satisfied after using our grievance process, the DPDP Act provides for complaints to the Data Protection Board of India.

### Your duties

Please give us accurate and authentic information, and use your rights responsibly.

## Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including access controls, encryption and internal security protocols. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected for, or for as long as the law requires. After that, it is securely deleted or archived in line with statutory requirements.

## Breach notification

If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify the affected individuals and the Data Protection Board of India, where required, within the timelines set under the DPDP Act.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date shown on this page tells you when it was last revised.

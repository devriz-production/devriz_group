If something has gone wrong in your dealings with Devriz Group or one of its companies, we want to know. This page explains who can raise a grievance, how to submit it and what happens next.

This process is not for emergencies. In a medical emergency, contact a doctor or the nearest hospital straight away.

## Who can raise a grievance

Anyone affected by the conduct of Devriz Group or a group company can raise a grievance, including:

- customers
- patients
- students and trainees
- employees and job applicants
- vendors, contractors and partners
- members of the public

## What you can raise

You can raise a concern about a product or service, the conduct of our staff or representatives, safety, billing, how your personal data has been handled, a suspected breach of our Code of conduct or Supplier code of conduct, the content of this website, or any other matter where you believe we have fallen short.

## What to include

A clear grievance is easier to resolve. Please include:

- your name and how we can reach you
- the group company, location or team involved
- what happened, when and where
- any reference, such as an invoice, order, booking or application number
- copies of supporting documents, messages or photographs
- the outcome you are looking for

If you send a grievance without your name or contact details, we will still read it, but we may not be able to look into it fully or reply to you.

## What happens next

1. **Acknowledge.** We confirm that we have received your grievance and note it on our record.
2. **Review.** The Grievance Officer, Devriz Group, reviews the matter, involves the relevant group company or team and may ask you for more information.
3. **Respond.** We reply with what we found, what we will do about it and the reasons for our decision.
4. **Escalate.** If you are not satisfied, reply to our response and ask for an escalation. The matter is then reviewed again at a more senior level in the group.

## Our targets

- We aim to acknowledge a grievance within 2 working days.
- We aim to give a substantive response within 15 working days.
- For requests about your personal data rights, we aim to respond within 30 days, as set out in our Privacy policy.

These are targets, not guarantees. Some matters take longer, for example where we need records from a third party. If that happens, we will tell you.

## Confidentiality and non-retaliation

- We share the details of a grievance only with the people who need them to review and resolve it, or where the law requires it.
- You can ask us to keep your identity confidential. We will respect that as far as a fair review and the law allow.
- No one will be treated unfairly for raising a grievance in good faith. Retaliation by any employee or representative of the group is a breach of our Code of conduct.

## When to contact a group company directly

Group companies are separate legal entities, and each is responsible for its own products and services. It is usually quicker to contact the company you dealt with first, for example about:

- an appointment, treatment or product from a healthcare or wellness company
- a course, fee or certificate from an academy
- an order, delivery or invoice
- a matter on a construction or project site

Contact the group through this page if you do not know which company to approach, if you have had no reply, or if you are not satisfied with the reply you received.

Some matters follow their own process under the law. Complaints of sexual harassment at the workplace are handled by the Internal Committee of the relevant group company under the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013.

## Your legal rights

This process is offered in addition to your rights under the law. It does not limit or replace any legal remedy. You remain free to approach a court, a consumer forum, a regulator or any other authority.

Fraudsters sometimes pose as employers, recruiters or HR staff and use the names of real organisations to take money or personal details from job seekers. If you are contacted about a job at Devriz Group or one of its companies, please read this page before you reply or pay anything.

## We never ask candidates for money

Devriz Group and its companies do not charge candidates at any stage of hiring. We never ask for:

- a registration or application fee
- a security deposit
- a training fee
- a charge for a laptop, uniform or other equipment
- any other payment in return for a job, an interview or an offer letter

If someone asks you to pay for a job with us, it is not a genuine offer. Do not pay.

## How genuine hiring works

Our hiring follows these steps:

1. **Apply.** You apply for a role, for example through the careers page on devrizgroup.com.
2. **HR screening.** Our HR team reviews your application and may contact you with questions.
3. **Interview or task.** You attend an interview, complete a task, or both.
4. **Selection.** If you are selected, you receive an offer.

An offer comes only after an interview. We do not make offers to people we have not interviewed, and we do not issue offer letters in exchange for a payment.

## How to verify a message

- **Check the sender's address.** Devriz Group's official email domain is devrizgroup.com. Be careful with messages sent from free email services, or from addresses that look similar to ours but are spelt differently.
- **Call the corporate office.** Phone +91 97737 79315 and ask whether the vacancy, the interview call or the offer is genuine.
- **Use the official website.** Type https://devrizgroup.com into your browser yourself. Do not rely on a link in a message.
- **If you are unsure, ask first.** If a message claims to come from a group company and you cannot confirm it, check with the corporate office before you reply.

## Warning signs

Treat a message as suspicious if:

- it asks for money in any form, including a refundable deposit
- it offers a job without an interview
- it tells you to pay into a personal bank account, a UPI ID or a wallet
- it comes only through a messaging app or social media, with no way to verify the sender
- it presses you to act immediately or to keep the offer secret
- it asks for your bank details, card number, OTP, PIN or passwords
- it asks for copies of identity documents before any interview has taken place
- the offer letter has spelling mistakes, an unclear logo or contact details that do not match those on this page
- the salary or benefits are far above what the role would normally pay

## What to do if you were approached

1. **Do not pay.** Do not send money, and do not share an OTP, PIN, password or bank details.
2. **Keep the evidence.** Save messages, emails, phone numbers, account details, payment receipts and screenshots.
3. **Tell us.** Email [info@devrizgroup.com](mailto:info@devrizgroup.com) with the subject line "Recruitment fraud report" and attach what you have.
4. **Report it to the authorities.** File a complaint on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in), or call the helpline 1930. You can also go to your local police station.
5. **If you have already paid, act quickly.** Contact your bank or payment provider at once, and call 1930.

Reporting to us helps us warn other job seekers. We cannot recover money for you, so please report to the authorities as well.

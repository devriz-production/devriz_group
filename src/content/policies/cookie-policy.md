This website keeps things simple. It sets no advertising cookies and no analytics cookies. The only thing it saves in your browser is a small set of display preferences, so that the site looks the way you chose when you come back.

This policy explains what is stored, why, and how you can remove it.

## The short version

- No advertising cookies.
- No analytics or tracking cookies.
- Three display preferences saved in your browser's local storage.
- Those preferences stay on your device. They are never sent to us.

## Cookies and local storage

A cookie is a small text file that a website places on your device. Cookies are usually sent back to the website's server with each page request, which is how many sites recognise returning visitors.

Local storage is a similar feature built into your browser. It lets a website save small pieces of information on your device. Unlike cookies, items in local storage are not sent to the server with page requests.

This website uses local storage only, and only for the items listed below.

## What we store

The website saves up to three items in local storage:

- `dg-theme`: remembers whether you chose the light or the dark colour theme.
- `dg-contrast`: remembers whether you turned on high-contrast mode.
- `dg-text-size`: remembers the text size you chose with the A-, A and A+ controls.

All three are preference settings only. They hold no name, contact detail or other personal data, and they are not used to identify or track you. They stay in your browser until you clear them.

There is one cookie, and visitors never receive it. When a member of Devriz Group staff signs in to the site's admin page to post a career opening or a newsletter, their browser is given a session cookie named `dg_admin`. It keeps them signed in, is removed when they sign out or close the browser, and is not set for anyone who has not signed in.

## What we do not do

- We do not use cookies or local storage for advertising.
- We do not run analytics or visitor-tracking tools on this website.
- We do not build profiles of visitors or follow you across other websites.
- We do not read your display preferences on our servers. They never leave your device.

## Embedded third-party content

Some pages can show content from another provider, for example a map on the contact page. This content is not loaded automatically. It loads only if you choose to open it.

When you open it, the provider may set its own cookies or collect information under its own policies, which we do not control. If you would rather avoid this, do not open the embedded content. Our office addresses are also given as plain text in the Contact section below.

## Server logs

Separately from cookies, the servers that host the website may keep standard logs of the IP address, browser type and pages requested. These logs are used for security. Our Privacy policy explains this.

## How to clear your preferences

You can change your theme, contrast and text size at any time with the display controls on the website.

To remove the stored items completely, clear the site data for devrizgroup.com in your browser:

- **Chrome and Edge**: open Settings, go to the privacy section and use the option to delete browsing data or to view and delete site data.
- **Firefox**: open Settings, go to Privacy and Security and use Manage Data under Cookies and Site Data.
- **Safari**: open Settings, go to Privacy and use Manage Website Data.

Menu names differ a little between browser versions and devices. Look for "site data", "cookies and site data" or "website data". Clearing this data also removes local storage.

After you clear the data, the website returns to its default appearance. If you browse in a private or incognito window, the preferences are removed when you close that window.

## Changes to this policy

If we add analytics or any other cookies in future, we will update this policy first, before they go live. The date shown on this page tells you when the policy was last revised.
